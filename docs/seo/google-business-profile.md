## 5. Review Acquisition & Management

### The 5-Star Protocol:
- Send review requests immediately upon successful live deployment milestone sign-off.
- Provide a direct shortlink generated from the GBP dashboard: `https://g.page/r/[ID]/review`.
- Request that clients mention specific project deliverables in their review (e.g. "bespoke web design", "Shopify migration", "Blackburn", "Preston").
