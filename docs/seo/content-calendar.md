# 12-Month SEO & Editorial Content Calendar

## Strategic Focus
This editorial calendar targets secondary, supporting, and question-based search queries identified in our Semrush keyword research. Each article is engineered to establish domain topical authority, solve a specific technical or commercial challenge for UK business leaders, and funnel organic visitors into our lead generation audits and bespoke proposal workflows.

---

## Content Production Guidelines
- **Word Count**: 1,200 – 1,800 words of authentic technical analysis.
- **Tone**: Engineering-led, practical, authoritative, commercial.
- **Structure**:
  - H1 containing primary topic query
  - Immediate value summary within first 75 words
  - Visual diagram, comparison table, or data snippet
  - Technical deep-dive with code / configuration snippets
  - Commercial implications for business owners
  - Clear CTA to `/free-website-audit/` or `/get-a-quote/`
