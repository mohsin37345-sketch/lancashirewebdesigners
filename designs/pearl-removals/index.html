How much does a house removal cost?

Most local 1–3 bedroom moves cost between £299 and £899. We give you a fixed, all-inclusive price up front based on volume, distance and access — so the price never changes on the day.

How far in advance should I book?

2–3 weeks is ideal, especially for Fridays and month-ends. We often have last-minute availability too — call us and we'll do our best.

Are my belongings insured?

Yes. Every move includes goods-in-transit cover up to £50,000 and public liability insurance as standard.

Do you provide boxes and packing materials?

We do. Choose a full packing service, or we can deliver boxes, tape and bubble wrap ahead of your move day.

Do you dismantle and reassemble furniture?

Yes — beds, wardrobes, tables and more are dismantled, wrapped and reassembled at your new home at no extra cost on our House Move plan.
